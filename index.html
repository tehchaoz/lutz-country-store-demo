Our story

Slow down, you're on country time.

Lutz Country Store and Cafe is where Highway 41 takes a breath. Come hungry for Cuban French toast, biscuits and gravy, pastrami hash and breakfast sandwiches built your way, with fresh lattes and lemonades in flavors like lavender and hibiscus.

Grab a seat on the porch under the string lights, or order ahead and swing by the window.
